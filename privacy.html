1. Introduction

HomeGoods ("HomeGoods", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect personal information when you visit homegoods.co.zw, browse our product catalogue, or contact us about our products and services.

Our website provides an online catalogue and enquiry service. Visitors can browse home essentials and contact us to ask about products, prices, availability and purchasing arrangements. The website does not currently offer online checkout or payment processing.

Where applicable law requires consent or another lawful basis for processing personal information, we will obtain or rely on the appropriate basis. Visiting this website does not, by itself, constitute consent to every type of data processing.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

When you submit an enquiry or contact us, you may provide:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone number.
  • The subject and content of your enquiry.
  • The products you are interested in.
  • Other information you choose to include in your message.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and the relevant processing arrangements have been explained to you.

3.2 Messaging and Telephone Communications

If you contact us by telephone, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger or another communication service, we may receive the information you choose to share, including your name, contact details and message content.

These services are operated by third parties and may process your information under their own terms and privacy policies.

3.3 Technical Information

Our hosting provider or other technical service providers may process information necessary to operate, maintain and secure the website. Depending on the services configured, this may include your IP address, browser and device information, requested pages, access times and technical error or security logs.

4. How We Use Your Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to product and service enquiries.
  • Provide information about prices and availability.
  • Communicate with you about an enquiry you submitted.
  • Maintain appropriate customer-service and business records.
  • Operate, maintain and troubleshoot the website.
  • Protect our website, users and business from misuse.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations and handle legal claims.

We aim to collect and use information that is adequate, relevant and necessary for the purpose concerned.

5. Lawful Basis for Processing

Where required by applicable law, we will identify and rely on an appropriate lawful basis for processing personal information. Depending on the circumstances, this may include your consent, taking steps at your request before entering into an arrangement, compliance with a legal obligation, or another basis permitted by law.

An enquiry about a product does not automatically mean that you have agreed to unrelated marketing or non-essential tracking.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent subject to applicable law. Withdrawal does not automatically make earlier lawful processing unlawful.

6. Contact Forms and Enquiries

Our website provides a contact form intended to let visitors send enquiries to HomeGoods. We use enquiry information to respond to messages and manage related customer-service activities.

The contact form's technical destination and processing method must be confirmed. Submissions may be handled through email, a third-party form provider, a website backend or another service. The final policy must accurately identify the arrangement used.

Submitting a product enquiry does not, by itself, subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Google Analytics is planned for possible future use but is not confirmed as installed or active at the time of publication.

If analytics or other non-essential tracking technologies are introduced, we will update this policy to describe their purposes, the information involved and the choices available to visitors.

Where required, appropriate consent or preference controls will be implemented before non-essential tracking is activated. Hosting providers may also use technical mechanisms necessary to deliver or secure the website.

8. Marketing Communications

HomeGoods has not yet confirmed whether it will offer a newsletter or promotional email service.

If we introduce marketing communications, we will explain how contact details are collected and used for that purpose and provide an appropriate way to opt out, as required by law.

9. Sharing Your Information

We do not intend to sell personal information to third parties. We may share information where reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including with:

  • Website hosting and technical service providers.
  • Email and contact-form providers.
  • Communication platforms you choose to use.
  • Professional advisers where reasonably necessary.
  • Public authorities or other parties where required or permitted by applicable law.

We aim to limit disclosures to information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. Third-party services may process information under their own privacy policies.

10. International Transfers

Some service providers may process or store information outside Zimbabwe. This may depend on the hosting, email, form or messaging services used.

We will assess relevant providers, processing locations and applicable legal requirements for international transfers of personal information, and take the measures required by law.

11. Data Retention

We will retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate recordkeeping requirements.

This may include:

  • Enquiry records needed to respond and manage follow-up.
  • Business records that must be retained by law.
  • Technical and security logs needed for operational purposes.

We will establish appropriate retention periods and deletion procedures for the systems we use, taking account of applicable legal requirements.

12. Data Security

We will take reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction.

Depending on the systems used, these measures may include restricted access, secure account credentials, software updates, secure website connections and appropriate backups.

No website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We will handle security incidents in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law and the circumstances of your request, you may have the right to:

  • Receive information about how your personal information is used.
  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or misleading information.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Request deletion where legally available.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Make a complaint to the relevant authority where appropriate.

These rights may be subject to lawful exceptions and limitations.

To make a request, email sales@homegoods.co.zw or call +263 77 492 3686. Please explain your request clearly. We may need to verify your identity before providing information or making changes.

We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

14. Children's Information

Our website is intended for people seeking information about HomeGoods products and services. It is not designed to deliberately collect children's personal information.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in circumstances where it should not have been collected, please contact us so that we can assess the matter and take appropriate steps under applicable law.

15. Third-Party Links

Our website may include links or buttons that open third-party services, including social media and messaging platforms. We do not control those services' privacy practices, content or security.

We encourage you to review the relevant privacy policies before sharing personal information with external services.

16. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our business, website functionality, service providers or applicable legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page with its effective or last-updated date. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent for material changes or new processing activities.
